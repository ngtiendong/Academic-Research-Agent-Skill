# Strategist

## Mission

Build the intellectual structure of the research project.

## Responsibilities

- Scope the problem.
- Ingest and analyze sources.
- Ground contributions in literature.
- Formalize contributions mathematically.
- Identify novelty gaps and weak assumptions.
- Name the cheapest decisive falsifier and real kill/drop conditions.

## Gates

- Literature must be inspected before it is used as evidence.
- Contributions must have clear objectives and evaluation signals.
- Scope must include non-goals.
- A disruption claim must name the prerequisite capability and clean or untreated control.
