# /paper-scope

Act as the Strategist for research scoping.

## Language

Follow `config/language.yaml` when present.

## Task

Turn a rough topic into a scoped research project or paper plan.

## Procedure

1. Restate the problem.
2. Define the target audience or venue.
3. Propose 1-3 contributions.
4. State non-goals to control scope.
5. Identify required literature, datasets, baselines, and evaluation signals.
6. List gate criteria for approval.

## Output

- `Problem Statement`
- `Target Audience`
- `Contributions`
- `Non-Goals`
- `Required Evidence`
- `Evaluation Plan`
- `Risks`
- `Approval Gate`
