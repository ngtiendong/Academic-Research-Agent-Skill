# 02_Scope

## Problem Statement

Many research ideas start too broad. This artifact narrows a topic into a researchable question, explicit contributions, non-goals, and evidence requirements.

## Research Question

How can we design a human-guided agent workflow that improves literature grounding and novelty checking for early-stage academic research?

## Candidate Contributions

1. A gate-based workflow for human-agent research collaboration.
2. A novelty gate that blocks shallow "method A applied to problem B" claims.
3. A traceable artifact pipeline connecting sources, formalization, experiments, and claims.

These are candidate contributions, not validated novelty claims.

## Non-Goals

- Fully autonomous paper generation.
- Replacing advisor or reviewer judgment.
- Claiming venue acceptance.

## Required Evidence

- Inspected direct competitors for the proposed workflow and gate design.
- A real research task with traceable source, decision, and result artifacts.
- A comparison showing whether the gates detect a consequential failure that an unstructured workflow misses.

## Cheapest Decisive Falsifier

Apply the Reality Gate to one real, apparently complete research plan. Stop if the gate cannot expose a testable fatal assumption or change the authorized next action.

## Kill Conditions

- The workflow only adds documentation without changing a research decision.
- Direct competitors already provide the same gate behavior and traceability.
- The claimed benefit cannot be measured on a real research task.

## Current Gate

`NOT_ASSESSED`: direct competitors and a real evaluation unit have not yet been inspected.

## Researcher Decision

Approve, revise, or reject this candidate scope before literature grounding. Approval authorizes evidence collection, not implementation or claims of effectiveness.
