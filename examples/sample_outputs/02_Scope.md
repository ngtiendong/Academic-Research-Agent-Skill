# 02_Scope

## Problem Statement

Many research ideas start too broad. This artifact narrows a topic into a researchable question, explicit contributions, non-goals, and evidence requirements.

## Research Question

How can we design a human-guided agent workflow that improves literature grounding and novelty checking for early-stage academic research?

## Candidate Contributions

1. A gate-based workflow for human-agent research collaboration.
2. A novelty gate that blocks shallow "method A applied to problem B" claims.
3. A traceable artifact pipeline connecting sources, formalization, experiments, and claims.

## Non-Goals

- Fully autonomous paper generation.
- Replacing advisor or reviewer judgment.
- Claiming venue acceptance.

## Researcher Decision

Approve, revise, or reject the proposed scope before literature grounding.
