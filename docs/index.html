Not a paper factory

Designed for human-machine research collaboration.

Research Agent Skill helps students use AI as a structured research partner: the agent drafts, extracts, challenges, plans, and verifies; the researcher decides, learns, revises, and approves. It is built for traceable research artifacts, not opaque chat transcripts.

Research lifecycle

From rough idea to evidence-traced plan.

1Idea

Capture intent, constraints, and research taste.

2Scope gate

Define question, contribution, and non-goals.

3Sources

Inspect papers, code, datasets, and notes.

4Grounding

Compare against closest prior work and baselines.

5Formalization

Define objects, objectives, assumptions, and metrics.

6Novelty gate

Block shallow method-on-domain contributions.

7Execution plan

Prepare risk plan, pilot, code plan, and artifacts.

8Review

Simulate reviewers and verify every important claim.

Who it helps

Master students

PhD students

Technical disciplines

Positioning

Honest by design.

Not this

  • AI writes your paper alone
  • Fake citations or fabricated results
  • One prompt to accepted paper
  • Hidden assumptions in chat history

This

  • Human approval gates
  • Evidence before claims
  • Novelty before implementation
  • Artifacts you can review and learn from

Start in minutes

Clone the skill and ask your AI agent to follow the workflow.

git clone https://github.com/YOUR_USERNAME/research-agent-skill
cd research-agent-skill
cp config/language.example.yaml config/language.yaml

Read SKILL.md, CLAUDE.md, and my_topic.md.
