Abstract

Academic Research Agent Skill is a reusable workflow for students who want to collaborate with AI agents without delegating research judgment to the machine. It structures early-stage technical research around explicit artifacts and gates: scope definition, source inspection, literature grounding, mathematical formalization, novelty review, experiment planning, reviewer simulation, and claim verification.

The skill is intentionally human-guided. Agents expand the student's capacity to read, compare, critique, plan, and verify; the student defines goals, makes decisions, approves scope, judges novelty, and owns final claims. The result is a traceable research process suitable for Master thesis work, PhD projects, and technical research training.

It fits Claude skill for PhD student workflows, Claude skills for research paper planning, agent research skills, academic research agent use cases, and auto research paper alternatives where evidence and human approval still matter.

Key Ideas

1

Human approval gates

Scope, novelty, pilot execution, and claims require explicit student approval.

2

Evidence before claims

Sources must be inspected before they support literature or method claims.

3

Research artifacts

Each step creates durable outputs that can be reviewed, revised, and reused.

Tool-Assisted Paper Ingestion

The workflow is intended for real technical literature review, where papers are not only discussed in chat but processed into inspectable artifacts. Academic Research Agent Skill supports a tool layer for downloading PDFs, converting papers to Markdown, reading figures and tables, and building source analysis matrices.

Paper downloader

Collect PDFs from source lists, arXiv links, DOI pages, or curated reading lists.

PDF-to-Markdown

Convert dense papers into searchable notes that agents and students can inspect.

Figure/table reader

Extract captions, crops, and surrounding context for visual evidence analysis.

Source matrix

Compare papers by problem, method, dataset, metric, result, limitation, and relevance.

Research Workflow

The workflow follows the structure of an academic project page rather than a product landing page. Each stage corresponds to a research artifact and a decision point.

Human-Agent Protocol

The protocol separates researcher responsibilities from agent responsibilities. This prevents the common failure mode where generated text looks polished but hides weak novelty, missing baselines, or unsupported claims.

  • The student defines research intent and constraints.
  • The agent prepares artifacts, alternatives, and critiques.
  • The student approves gates before moving forward.
  • Claims are linked to inspected sources or experiment artifacts.

Artifacts

The skill turns conversation into research artifacts. These files make progress inspectable by the student, advisor, lab, or a future agent session.

Artifact Purpose Gate
02_Scope.md Research question, contributions, assumptions, non-goals. Scope approval
05_Lit_Grounding.md Closest prior work, baselines, and evidence matrix. Literature readiness
paper_notes/*.md PDF-to-Markdown notes generated from downloaded papers. Source inspection
figure_tables/ Figure/table crops, captions, and visual evidence analysis. Visual evidence review
06_Math_Formalization.md Definitions, objectives, constraints, and implementation mapping. Formal clarity
Novelty Gate Report Harsh review of whether the contribution is more than method-on-domain. Continue, revise, or stop
12_Code_Execution_Plan.md Interfaces, datasets, baselines, metrics, pilots, and reproducibility. Pilot approval
Claim Verification Trace each factual claim to a source, formal artifact, or result. Draft readiness

Learning Loop

The target user is not a passive prompt sender. The skill is designed to help students learn the shape of research by repeatedly drafting, critiquing, grounding, deciding, and revising.

Getting Started

Clone the repository and ask your AI assistant to follow the skill instructions.

git clone https://github.com/ngtiendong/Academic-Research-Agent-Skill
cd Academic-Research-Agent-Skill
cp config/language.example.yaml config/language.yaml

Then ask:

Read SKILL.md, CLAUDE.md, and my_topic.md.
Use Academic Research Agent Skill to help me scope this research idea.
Do not skip human decision gates.
