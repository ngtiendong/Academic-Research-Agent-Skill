# Positioning

## Recommended Name

Research Agent Skill

## Tagline

A human-guided research agent for Master and PhD students in technical fields such as computer science, AI, mathematics, and engineering.

## One-Liner

Research Agent Skill helps Master, PhD, and independent researchers in technical disciplines collaborate with AI agents across literature review, novelty checking, math formalization, experiment planning, reviewer simulation, and claim verification.

## Core Message

This workflow does not ask agents to replace researchers. It helps researchers use agents as collaborators:

- to read and organize more sources,
- to explore more ideas,
- to see weak assumptions earlier,
- to learn research structure by doing,
- to plan experiments more carefully,
- to produce traceable artifacts instead of opaque chat transcripts.

## Strong Positioning

> Not "AI writes your paper."  
> Research Agent Skill helps you become a better researcher with an agent team that reads, challenges, plans, and verifies under your direction.

## Audience

Primary:

- Master students.
- PhD students.
- Students in computer science, AI, machine learning, mathematics, engineering, and related technical fields.
- Early-career researchers.
- Research engineers.

Secondary:

- Advisors and labs evaluating AI-assisted research workflows.
- Builders of AI research assistant tools.

## Differentiation

| Category | Position |
|---|---|
| Autonomous research agents | More honest, more collaborative, less overclaiming. |
| Deep research/report tools | More academic: novelty, formalization, experiments, claims. |
| Prompt packs | More structured: roles, gates, artifacts, workflows. |
| Writing assistants | Earlier in the pipeline: idea to evidence to plan to review. |
